Why this exists

You read. You highlight. You forget.

Most reading apps are great at helping you capture insights. None of them bring those insights back. The Reading Room surfaces one passage at a time, randomly, whenever you have a quiet moment — turning your highlights into something you actually benefit from instead of a graveyard of good intentions.

It's the opposite of doomscrolling. A finite shelf. Your own words, returning to you.

What it does

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

📖
Import from anywhere
Kindle's My Clippings.txt, Goodreads notes pages, PDFs. Your whole library in one import.
🎲
Random review
One passage at a time, at random. A finite shelf — not an endless feed.
🏷
Books & themes
Filter by book or tag by theme across all your books — #Stoicism, #Money, #GrowthMindset.
📝
Your own notes
Add a note to any passage — why it stuck, what it changed. Shown right alongside.
🔍
Search
Find any passage across your entire shelf instantly, with matching words highlighted.
☁️
Optional sync
Highlights live on your device by default. Optional free sync across phone and laptop via Supabase.

Install

Add it to your home screen.

The Reading Room is a Progressive Web App. No app store, no install fee, full offline support. Add it to your home screen and it works exactly like a native app.
